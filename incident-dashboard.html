Reading This Data

Interpretation & Data Quality Notes

  • Medical response is the core workload. 247 reports (50.5%) are injury/illness/medical — this is as much a first-aid operation as a security one. 88 incidents triggered a 911 call and 50 ended in a hospital transport.
  • Serious security events are rare but present: 4 weapons-related, 2 violence/threat, 2 inappropriate-touching/sexual-assault, 4 mental-health, plus 14 unauthorized-entry reports over the period.
  • Escalation is well-documented: Safety & Security was notified in 368 of 489 reports (75%) and police in 198 (41%).
  • Data hygiene: the Department field is blank on every row. "Nature" and "Location" contain free-text duplicates (e.g. "Suspicious Person" vs "Suspicious Individual"; several "sanctuary" spellings) that were merged for this view. One incident date (1963) is an obvious mis-entry and is excluded from the trend.
  • Under-completion of follow-up fields: "911 called" and "transported to hospital" are blank on more than half of reports, so response rates are shown against reports where the field was actually answered.
