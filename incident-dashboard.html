Reading This Data

Interpretation & Data Quality Notes

  • Medical response is the core workload. Injury/illness/medical is the single largest category every year — this is as much a first-aid operation as a security one.
  • 2026 shifts toward security events: weapons (4), suspicious individuals (10), mental-health (4) and violence/threat reports concentrate in the current year — worth watching alongside the medical baseline.
  • Escalation is well-documented: Safety & Security was notified in 75% of all reports and police in 41%.
  • Data hygiene: the Department field is blank on every row; "Nature" and "Location" contain free-text duplicates (merged for this view); one incident date (1963) is an obvious mis-entry excluded from the trend. 2023 (from July) and Aug 2026 are partial months.
  • Under-completed follow-up fields: "911 called" and "transported to hospital" are blank on more than half of reports, so response rates are shown against reports where the field was actually answered.
