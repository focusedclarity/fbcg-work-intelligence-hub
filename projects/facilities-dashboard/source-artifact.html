What the data says

Five takeaways from 196 inspections and 268 documented findings, January through July.

1

Winter showed up in the pavement, then eased. Parking lots and sidewalks were the single largest issue source in H1. Exterior issues peaked in February (19) and were worked down through spring — the summer months run far lighter.

2

The program keeps proving itself. Findings per inspection have fallen steadily — 2.2 (Feb) → 1.8 → 1.2 → 1.0 → 1.1 (Jun) → 0.6 in July: catch, fix, and find less on the next pass.

3

Two programs, two profiles. Building checks carry the workload (240 findings, 37% clean). Environmental checks — temperature and climate conditions in equipment spaces — stay healthy (28 findings, 77% clean).

4

Coverage is slipping at two campuses. Worship Center, Ministry Center and the Community Life Center were all walked at the end of July, but the Empowerment Center hasn’t been walked since June 18 and the Service Building since June 4 — 43 and 57 days as of July 31.

5

The inspection-to-fix loop is real but uneven. Of 110 FMX work orders our inspections logged in H1, 65% were closed by June 30 (median ~11 days). Ministry Center closes at 76% and Worship Center at 50%, but the Community Life Center has 0 of 8 closed.
