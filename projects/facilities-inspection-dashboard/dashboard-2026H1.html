What the data says

Five takeaways from 170 inspections and 253 documented findings.

1

Winter showed up in the pavement. Parking lots and sidewalks were the single largest issue source (34 checkpoint failures). Exterior issues peaked in February (19) and were worked down to 3 by June.

2

The program is proving itself. Findings per inspection fell from 2.2 in February to 1.1 in June at full cadence — catch, fix, and find less on the next pass.

3

Two programs, two profiles. Building checks carry the workload (225 findings, 39% clean). Environmental checks show healthy equipment spaces (28 findings, 71% clean).

4

Interior wear tracks foot traffic. After exterior, failures cluster in restrooms, the sanctuary, and stairwells (23 each) — the highest-traffic, most member-facing spaces.

5

Ministry Center got the deepest look. 124 of 253 findings came from its 23 inspections. When the FMX export lands, the next question is how fast that pipeline clears.
